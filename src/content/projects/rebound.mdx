<p id="journey" class="eyebrow collection-label">User journey</p>

<SectionTitle>Annie’s Road to Recovery</SectionTitle>

Annie’s recovery runs through five stages. We focused the design on mid rehab, when appointments thin out, home exercises get repetitive, and motivation drops the most.
